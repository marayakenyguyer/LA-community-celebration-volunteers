About the Events

World Cup Community Celebrations

From June 11 to July 19, 2026, the City will host daily World Cup Community Celebrations rotating across all 15 Council Districts. Three activations per match day bring the energy of the tournament directly into our parks — making this global moment feel local.

📺

Live Match Screenings

Watch 2026 FIFA World Cup matches together on big screens at local Recreation & Parks sites.

⚽

Youth Soccer Clinics

Skills activities and soccer clinics for young Angelenos inspired by the world's greatest players.

🎉

Family Fun Zones

Family programming, music, fan engagement, and neighborhood gathering for all ages.

🏙️

Community Resources

City resource tables and local programming connecting Angelenos to services and opportunities.

Celebrations will take place at Recreation & Parks sites citywide. Council Districts may also incorporate local music, food vendors, and additional neighborhood programming.

Get Involved

Volunteer Roles

We are recruiting two types of volunteers to power LA's World Cup Community Celebrations.

High Commitment  ·  District-Based

District Lead Volunteer Coordinator

2 per Council District  ·  30–45 total citywide

What You'll Do
  • ✓Serve as the consistent on-site leader for all celebrations in your Council District
  • ✓Welcome and guide attendees; help create an inclusive, positive atmosphere
  • ✓Support and organize general volunteers during each activation
  • ✓Help troubleshoot minor issues and escalate concerns to City staff
  • ✓Represent the City and your neighborhood with professionalism and pride
Time Commitment
All 7 activation days in your Council District — approximately one every five days throughout the tournament. You'll serve a half-day shift per activation, rotating with your co-lead.
Who Should Apply
  • ✓Community leaders with demonstrated volunteer or civic experience
  • ✓Dependable, organized, comfortable in public-facing roles
  • ✓People who care deeply about their neighborhood
Flexible  ·  Citywide

General Volunteer

One shift or many  ·  All backgrounds welcome

What You'll Do
  • ✓Greet attendees, assist with ticketing, and provide wayfinding
  • ✓Support activities and fan engagement areas
  • ✓Assist with event set-up and breakdown
  • ✓Assist with programming or tabling at community resource areas
  • ✓Support accessibility and inclusive engagement for all attendees
Time Commitment
Flexible — sign up for one shift or multiple shifts across the tournament. We'll aim to place you in your home district whenever possible.
Who Should Apply
  • ✓Community-minded Angelenos of all backgrounds
  • ✓No prior experience required — just enthusiasm and heart
  • ✓Anyone who wants to support youth, families, and their community

⚽  Volunteer FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about volunteering for LA's World Cup Community Celebrations.

Will I be paid? ▼
No. This is a volunteer opportunity and does not include compensation or tickets to official matches.
Is this the official FIFA Volunteer Program? ▼
No. This is a City of Los Angeles-run volunteer program supporting community celebrations at neighborhood Recreation & Parks sites — not official FIFA venues.
Is there a minimum shift requirement? ▼
General Volunteers may sign up for one or multiple shifts — it's flexible, and you're welcome to do as many as you'd like! District Lead Coordinators must commit to all seven events in their Council District.
Will I get to watch the games during the celebrations? ▼
Most volunteers will be stationed near match screenings and activities. While your primary role is to support attendees, we will do our best to ensure volunteers have opportunities to enjoy the atmosphere.
Will transportation be provided? ▼
No. Volunteers are responsible for their own transportation. We aim to place volunteers in their home district whenever possible and encourage transit, biking, or walking.
What if I have accessibility needs? ▼
We are committed to inclusion and accessibility. You may share accommodation needs in your application. If you require assistance completing the application, please contact maraya.kenyguyer@lacity.org.
Do I need to speak multiple languages? ▼
Volunteers should be comfortable communicating in English. Additional language skills are deeply appreciated and welcomed — Los Angeles is a multilingual city and we celebrate that.
Why should I volunteer? ▼
Celebrate your community, show civic pride, and be part of a historic global moment in LA. This program also serves as an early step toward future City-led events, including the 2028 Olympic and Paralympic Games.
I love volunteering — are there more City opportunities? ▼
Yes, we love that question! Explore additional City volunteer opportunities here. If you want to get involved locally but only have a few hours, we encourage you to join a ShineLA event in your neighborhood!

Still have questions? We're happy to help.
