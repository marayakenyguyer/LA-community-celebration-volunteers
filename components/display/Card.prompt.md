**Card** — the workhorse content container for roles, missions and journey steps. Soft elevation by default; `pop` for the bold ink-outlined sticker look used on marketing pages.

```jsx
<Card eyebrow="Mission" title="City Guidance" accent="sunset" interactive>
  Help visitors find their way through transit hubs and celebration sites.
</Card>
<Card image="/assets/photos/volunteers-sunset.jpg" eyebrow="Track 1" title="Volunteer Workforce" pop>
  Recruit, vet and staff leadership and general roles.
</Card>
```
Accent options include `rainbow` and `sunset` gradient bars; use sparingly as feature highlights.
